ABOUT US

Built around HVAC expertise and dependable service.

Modern National Tech SPC is an established and innovative air-conditioning (HVAC) and trading company founded in 2007 in Salalah, Sultanate of Oman.

We specialize in the supply, installation, testing and commissioning of HVAC works for residential, commercial and industrial projects, together with trading of air-conditioning materials.

MissionQuality design, installation and service for HVAC equipment.
VisionProfessional HVAC products and services with reliable installation and service.
ValuesCustomer satisfaction, openness, teamwork and continuous improvement.
